**Feat (slice 852): Banishment — CHA save → Incapacitated (concentration-bound) — closes `l4-banishment`**
The spell shipped `mechanicalEffects: []` — a cast did nothing. RAW: "must succeed on a Charisma saving throw or be transported to a harmless demiplane ... While there, the target has the Incapacitated condition. When the spell ends, the target reappears." RAW's only mechanical hook is the Incapacitated condition, so this is a thin wire: the mechanic is `{ save CHA, conditionOnFail: 'banished-active' }`; the new `banished-active` condition carries `effects: []` and the action-block is engine-coded by adding its id to `ACTION_BLOCKING_CONDITIONS` (`_actor-state.ts`) — the held-paralyzed-active / sleep-drowsy-active path.
