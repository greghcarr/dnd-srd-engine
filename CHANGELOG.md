**Fix (slice 816): Spiritual Weapon's immediate attack no longer costs the Action**
Closes the [L7 audit](docs/l7-completion-audit.md) `spiritual-weapon-immediate-attack-action-cost` divergence (surfaced by slice 815). `cast-spell.ts` charged a Bonus-Action spell *both* a Bonus Action and an Action when cast at a target — slice 603's model for a spell whose attack is a *separate* Magic action (Produce Flame "take a Magic action to hurl fire"). Now the immediate-on-cast attack costs only the Bonus Action; the separate-Magic-action hurl still costs both (slice-603 behavior preserved). New 4-test slice-816 (player-cleric Spiritual Weapon = BA only; druid Flame Blade = BA + Action; flag authored right; a durable invariant guarding the heuristic→flag switch); slice-815 #4 flipped to the fixed behavior. `tsc` clean; coverage/exports snapshots unchanged; fast suite green (598 files, 4583 passed).

**Fix (slice 804): Armor Training penalties (untrained armor)**
