**Feat (slice 866): over-capacity Speed cap (≤ 5 ft) — closes `over-capacity-speed-5`**
RAW (Carrying Capacity): "While dragging, lifting, or pushing weight in excess of the maximum weight you can carry, your Speed can be no more than 5 feet." Slice 865 gave `computeEncumbrance` a binary `overCapacity` flag but nothing consumed it. `getEffectiveSpeedForMode` (the chokepoint behind every speed alias) now applies the cap as its last step: when carried inventory weight exceeds the size×STR×15 carry max, every mode's effective Speed is clamped to ≤ 5 ft (`Math.min(uncapped, 5)`).
