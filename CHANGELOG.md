**Feat (slice 867): climb / swim / crawl movement-cost surcharge — closes `climb-swim-crawl-cost`**
RAW (Climbing / Swimming / Crawling): "each foot of movement costs 1 extra foot (2 extra feet in Difficult Terrain)." `planMove` accepted a `movementMode` but only `'fly'` did anything. It now charges a **+1 ft per geometric foot** surcharge for `'climb'` / `'swim'` / `'crawl'`. The Difficult-Terrain doubling falls out for free: the path cost already doubles Difficult-Terrain feet, so +1 per *geometric* foot reproduces the RAW total (normal 1+1=2/ft, difficult 2+1=3/ft); geometric distance = path step count (mapped) or Chebyshev (positionless). Climb/Swim **waive** the cost when the mover has the matching effective Climb/Swim Speed (`getEffectiveSpeedForMode > 0`); Crawl never waives. New `'crawl'` mode keeps the mover **Prone** — the RAW alternative to standing up, paying the +1 ft/ft surcharge instead of the half-speed stand-up (no `ConditionRemoved`). The surcharge counts against remaining movement (over-budget throws, naming it) and folds into `feetTraveled`. `MovementMode` gains `'crawl'` (snapshots track names, not union shape — unchanged). New 7-test slice-867 (walk base; climb/swim double without the matching speed; Giant Rat climb 30 exempt; Prone walk stands up + sheds Prone; Prone crawl stays Prone + crawl surcharge; over-budget climb throws). `tsc` clean; `test:fast` (645 files, 4846 passed — +1 file/+7 tests over 866, default walk/fly byte-identical, zero regression) + doc audits green.

**Feat (slice 866): over-capacity Speed cap (≤ 5 ft) — closes `over-capacity-speed-5`**
RAW (Carrying Capacity): "While dragging, lifting, or pushing weight in excess of the maximum weight you can carry, your Speed can be no more than 5 feet." Slice 865 gave `computeEncumbrance` a binary `overCapacity` flag but nothing consumed it. `getEffectiveSpeedForMode` (the chokepoint behind every speed alias) now applies the cap as its last step: when carried inventory weight exceeds the size×STR×15 carry max, every mode's effective Speed is clamped to ≤ 5 ft (`Math.min(uncapped, 5)`). RAW "your Speed" is general → walk and the non-walk modes are all capped; the cap only lowers (a 0-set Grappled/Restrained stays 0, a `set` override like Phantom Steed clamps down). `GetEffectiveSpeedInput` already carries `{ character, content, itemInstances }`, so no signature change; an empty inventory takes a no-weigh fast path. The Boots of Striding and Springing exemption is deferred (the item ships inert). New 8-test slice-866 (walk capped over/at/under the max; the empty-inventory fast path; `getEffectiveSpeeds.walk`; an over-capacity Ghost's fly 40 → 5; an under-capacity flyer keeps fly 40; the only-lowers invariant).
