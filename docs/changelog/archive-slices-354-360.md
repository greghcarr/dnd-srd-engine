**Content: Oath of Devotion Aura of Devotion ally-side half (slice 360)**

Pure content slice on the existing `GrantAura` primitive (shipped for the paladin's Aura of Protection / Aura of Courage). Completes the partial Aura of Devotion wire: the L7 feature already granted the paladin self-immunity to Charmed via `GrantConditionImmunity`; this adds the ally-projecting half by mirroring Aura of Courage exactly. The feature gains a `GrantAura { auraId: 'aura-of-devotion', rangeFeet: 10, allyConditionId: 'aura-of-devotion-active' }`, and a new `aura-of-devotion-active` condition (mirroring `aura-of-courage-active`) grants Charmed immunity to allies the aura lands on. RAW 2024: "You and your allies have Immunity to the Charmed condition while in your Aura of Protection." As with the other auras, the aura's metadata is self-describing and the actual in-range application is consumer territory (positions aren't modeled).

No engine change. New [tests/unit/engine/slice-360-aura-of-devotion.test.ts](../../tests/unit/engine/slice-360-aura-of-devotion.test.ts): the L7 paladin projects a 10-ft `aura-of-devotion` with the `aura-of-devotion-active` ally condition, the self Charmed-immunity is retained, the `aura-of-devotion-active` condition grants an ally Charmed immunity, and the aura does not project before L7. Coverage snapshot regenerated (+1 condition: `aura-of-devotion-active`).

Content audit (RAW match): the immunity (Charmed), the aura range (10 ft, the Aura of Protection range), and the self + ally coverage all match SRD 5.2.1 Aura of Devotion; the wiring is structurally identical to Aura of Courage (Frightened), the established sibling. No new primitive; `tsc --noEmit` clean; full suite green (310 files).

**Engine + content: Evoker Empowered Evocation + `event.spellSchool` damage fact (slice 359)**

New `event.spellSchool` fact on the cast-spell damage-modifier fold ([src/engine/plan/cast-spell.ts](../../src/engine/plan/cast-spell.ts)), exposed alongside the existing `event.damageType` on both the spell-attack damage path and the primary save-damage component (deliberately **not** the additional-damage loop, so a multi-type evocation spell like Flame Strike gets the bonus on only one roll). Canonical user: **Evoker L10 Empowered Evocation** (RAW 2024: "whenever you cast a Wizard spell from the Evocation school, you can add your Intelligence modifier to one damage roll of that spell"). Wired as a passive `AddModifier { target: 'damage', value: { abilityMod INT }, condition: { eq event.spellSchool == 'evocation' } }` on a new L10 `levelGrants` row on the Evoker subclass. Because `saveDamageModifierBonus` is folded once into the shared `rawDamage` before the per-target full/half split, an AoE save spell receives the +INT exactly once and applies it to every target, matching "one damage roll." No `Custom` marker / planner: it is a pure passive modifier, the same shape as Draconic Sorcery's Elemental Affinity (which gates on `event.damageType`).

New [tests/unit/engine/slice-359-empowered-evocation.test.ts](../../tests/unit/engine/slice-359-empowered-evocation.test.ts): an Evoker vs a plain wizard (same seed, INT 20, a guaranteed-failed save via a -5 target) casting Fireball, asserting the Evoker's total is exactly +5 (INT mod) higher; and the same comparison on Blight (necromancy) asserting **no** delta, proving the school gate. Coverage snapshot regenerated (+1 wired subclass feature: `evoker L10 empowered-evocation`).

Uncle Bob audit: **Names** `event.spellSchool` mirrors the existing `event.damageType` fact convention. **DRY** reuses the `modifierSum('damage', facts)` fold and the Elemental Affinity `AddModifier`-with-`event`-predicate content shape verbatim; the only engine change is adding one key to two existing fact maps. **SRP** the fold still computes one damage-modifier sum; the school is just another fact the caller's predicate can read. **Magic numbers** none introduced; the INT mod comes from the `abilityMod` formula in RAW-cited content. **at-threading** unchanged; no new RNG. **Mechanical outcomes asserted** the exact +INT-once on a failed-save evocation AoE and the zero-delta on a non-evocation spell (the school gate). **Tests** prevent the bonus leaking onto non-evocation spells and a regression in the "one damage roll" single-application. The deliberate omission of the fact from the additional-damage loop is documented inline. No em/en dashes. Full suite green (309 files), `tsc --noEmit` clean.

**Engine + content: College of Lore Peerless Skill (slice 358)**

New `planPeerlessSkill` ([src/engine/plan/peerless-skill.ts](../../src/engine/plan/peerless-skill.ts)) + `engine.plan.peerlessSkill`. RAW 2024 (College of Lore L14): when the bard makes an ability check or attack roll and fails, expend one Bardic Inspiration die, roll it, and add it to the d20, potentially turning a failure into a success; on a failure (still under the threshold), the Bardic Inspiration isn't expended. This is the self-targeted mirror of Cutting Words (L3, already wired): the consumer passes the already-failed total + the threshold it needed to meet, and the planner rolls the BI die, returns `turnedSuccess`, and emits `ResourceSpent` **only** when the boost lifts the roll to meet the threshold. Reuses `bardicInspirationDieFor` + `BARDIC_INSPIRATION_RESOURCE_ID` from the Cutting Words planner (both newly exported). A new L14 `levelGrants` row on the College of Lore subclass carries a `Custom { handlerId }` planner marker. Costs no action / reaction (RAW), so no `ActionEconomyConsumed` is emitted.

Returns a `PeerlessSkillOutcome` (`{ events, dieRoll, turnedSuccess }`), matching the Cutting Words convenience shape rather than the bare `PlanResult`, so the consumer can branch on whether to commit the trailing chain. New [tests/unit/engine/slice-358-peerless-skill.test.ts](../../tests/unit/engine/slice-358-peerless-skill.test.ts): the d12-at-L14 near-miss turned into a success (die spent), the badly-failed roll that the boost can't save (die **not** expended, zero events), and the subclass / level / no-Bardic-Inspiration guards. Coverage snapshot regenerated (+1 wired subclass feature: `college-of-lore L14 peerless-skill`).

Uncle Bob audit: **Names** `planPeerlessSkill` / `PeerlessSkillOutcome.turnedSuccess` read as the rulebook. **DRY** reuses the Cutting Words BI-die helper + resource id (exported rather than copied) and the same Outcome shape; the only new logic is the add-vs-subtract direction and the spend-only-on-success refund rule. **SRP** the planner validates + rolls + reports + conditionally emits one ResourceSpent; the consumer owns the d20 it already rolled. **Magic numbers** the L14 gate is a named constant; the BI die comes from the shared table. **at-threading** single `nowIso()`; the one die roll is the only RNG, consumed in the planner. **Mechanical outcomes asserted** the die range, the success/failure boundary behavior, the no-expend-on-failure refund, and the three guards. **Tests** prevent the die being spent on a roll it can't save (the RAW refund), a non-Lore or under-level bard using it, and use with no Bardic Inspiration. No em/en dashes. Full suite green (308 files), `tsc --noEmit` clean.

**Engine + content: Warrior of the Open Hand Wholeness of Body (slice 357)**

New `planWholenessOfBody` ([src/engine/plan/wholeness-of-body.ts](../../src/engine/plan/wholeness-of-body.ts)) + `engine.plan.wholenessOfBody`. RAW 2024 (Warrior of the Open Hand L6): as a Bonus Action, roll the Martial Arts die and regain Hit Points equal to the roll plus your Wisdom modifier (minimum 1); usable a number of times equal to your Wisdom modifier (min 1) per Long Rest. The feature gains a new L6 `levelGrants` row on the Warrior of the Open Hand subclass carrying a `GrantResource` (`wholeness-of-body`, max `max(1, WIS mod)`, recharge longRest, the per-LR uses cap) plus a `Custom { handlerId }` planner marker. The planner validates the subclass at monk level 6+, spends the resource (`ResourceSpent`), rolls the Martial Arts die (reusing `martialArtsDie` from the attack planner) + WIS modifier (min 1), and emits a self `Healed`. The Bonus Action is consumed only when the monk is the active combatant in an encounter (mirrors `planPreserveLife` / `planDragonWings`); out of combat the healing is simply applied, since self-healing is legitimate outside initiative.

This is the first wired Warrior of the Open Hand post-L3 feature. New [tests/unit/engine/slice-357-wholeness-of-body.test.ts](../../tests/unit/engine/slice-357-wholeness-of-body.test.ts): the resource spend + self-heal in the 1d8+WIS range at monk L6, the heal applied on commit (clamped to HP max), and the subclass / level / no-uses guards. Coverage snapshot regenerated (+1 wired subclass feature: `warrior-of-the-open-hand L6 wholeness-of-body`).

Uncle Bob audit: **Names** `planWholenessOfBody` matches the feature; `WHOLENESS_OF_BODY_LEVEL` / `_RESOURCE_ID` / `MIN_HP_REGAINED` / `OPEN_HAND_SUBCLASS_ID` named. **DRY** reuses `martialArtsDie` (the Monk-table die already used by Patient Defense / attack), `abilityModifier`, the `Healed` event + reducer, the `dark-ones-own-luck` `GrantResource` formula-max shape, and the `planPreserveLife` in/out-of-encounter gating + Sacred Weapon `Custom`-marker convention. **SRP** the planner validates + (conditionally) consumes the Bonus Action + spends the resource + emits one self-heal. **Magic numbers** the min-1 floor is named; the Martial Arts die comes from the shared helper; the WIS-mod uses cap lives in the RAW-cited `GrantResource` formula. **at-threading** single `nowIso()`; the single die roll is the only RNG, consumed in the planner. **Mechanical outcomes asserted** the resource spend, the heal range + on-commit application + HP-max clamp, and the three guards. **Tests** prevent a non-Open-Hand or under-level monk using it, use past the per-LR cap, and a regression in the heal amount. No em/en dashes. Full suite green (307 files), `tsc --noEmit` clean.

**Engine + content: Circle of the Land Land's Aid (slice 354)**

New `planLandsAid` ([src/engine/plan/lands-aid.ts](../../src/engine/plan/lands-aid.ts)) + `engine.plan.landsAid`. RAW 2024 (Circle of the Land L3): as a Magic action, expend a use of Wild Shape and choose a point within 60 ft; each chosen creature in a 10-ft-radius Sphere makes a Constitution save against the druid's spell save DC, taking 2d6 Necrotic on a failure or half on a success, and one chosen creature in the area regains 2d6 Hit Points. The damage and healing both scale to 3d6 at druid level 10 and 4d6 at level 14. The planner validates Circle of the Land at druid level 3+, spends the `wild-shape` resource (`ResourceSpent`), computes the spell save DC via `computeSpellSaveDC` (druids are full casters, unlike the barbarian feature-DC case in Intimidating Presence), rolls the Necrotic pool once and applies it full / half per target through the shared `rollSaveAgainstDC` + `mitigateDamage` + `interceptFatalDamage` pipeline (so per-type resistance and the drop-to-0 concentration break are honored as for any spell save), then emits a single `Healed` for the chosen creature. The subclass feature carries a `Custom { handlerId }` planner marker, preserving the invariant that every pack `Custom` handlerId pairs with an engine planner.

Action economy (a Magic action): consumed only when the druid is the active combatant in an encounter (mirrors `planPreserveLife`); out of combat the effect is simply applied. Range (60 ft) and area membership are consumer-supplied.

Closes the L3 Land's Aid stub. Seven of the original twelve L3 `effects: []` stubs are now closed (slices 346-354); the remaining five are Circle of the Land's nature cantrip (2014-PHB extra) + Circle Spells, Hunter's Lore (narrative), Thief Fast Hands, and Monk Open Hand Technique. New [tests/unit/engine/slice-354-lands-aid.test.ts](../../tests/unit/engine/slice-354-lands-aid.test.ts): the dice-pool scaling table (2d6 at L3-9, 3d6 at L10-13, 4d6 at L14+), the Wild Shape spend + per-target CON save (DC 15) + single heal, the full-on-fail / half-on-success shared-roll relationship, and the Circle-of-the-Land / Wild-Shape guards. Coverage snapshot regenerated (+1 wired subclass feature: `circle-of-the-land L3 lands-aid`).

Uncle Bob audit: **Names** `planLandsAid` / `landsAidDiceCount` match the feature; `LANDS_AID_BASE_DICE` / `LANDS_AID_SCALE_LEVEL_FIRST` / `_SECOND` / `WILD_SHAPE_RESOURCE_ID` named. **DRY** reuses `rollSaveAgainstDC`, `mitigateDamage`, `interceptFatalDamage`, `planConcentrationBreakOnDrop`, the `planPreserveLife` in/out-of-encounter gating, and the Sacred Weapon `Custom`-marker convention; `landsAidDiceCount` extracted + exported so the scaling table is unit-tested directly. **SRP** the planner validates + (conditionally) consumes the action + spends the resource + rolls one shared damage pool + emits the per-target mitigated damage + the single heal; the dice-count scaling is a small named helper. **Magic numbers** the 2d6 base + the L10/L14 +1d6 steps + the d6 size are RAW-cited named constants. **at-threading** single `nowIso()`; all RNG (the damage pool, each save, the heal pool) consumed in the planner, never a reducer. **Mechanical outcomes asserted** the scaling table, the resource spend, the save DC, the full/half relationship, and the guards. **Tests** prevent a regression in the scaling thresholds, the shared-roll half-on-success rule, and a non-qualifying actor or a druid out of Wild Shape uses using it. No em/en dashes. Full suite green (306 files), `tsc --noEmit` clean.
