**Feat (slice 802): Surprise gives Disadvantage on Initiative**
SRD 2024 surprise = Disadvantage on the Initiative roll, but `RollInitiativeIntent` had no surprise channel — an ambushee rolled straight. Added a consumer-coordinated `RollInitiativeIntent.surprisedCombatantIds?` (the engine has no awareness model, same shape as positions/LoS); `planRollInitiative` OR-s it into the combatant's initiative disadvantage, and advantage + surprise cancel to a straight roll. Omitting it is byte-unchanged. **Closes the [L7 audit](docs/l7-completion-audit.md) Area 4 divergence `surprise-not-in-initiative`** (the Incapacitated→disadvantage / Invisible→advantage glossary arms are a separate condition-driven follow-up). New 2-test slice-802; fast suite green.

**Fix (slice 801): distinct picks on multi-select choices (ASI back-door)**
The ASI "+1 to two ability scores" menu (`asi-plus1-abilities`, oneOf:2) accepted `['str','str']` — two +1s stacking into an illegal +2 to one ability, the back-door the separate +2-to-one choice exists for. Added a distinctness check (`new Set(ids).size === ids.length`) to both the planner gate (`planResolveChoice`) and the replay invariant (`applyChoiceResolved`), after the existing count + membership checks. One fix covers every multi-select `oneOf:N` choice (Skilled, Magic Initiate, …); single-select choices (the +2-to-one path) are unaffected. **Closes the [L7 audit](docs/l7-completion-audit.md) Area 5 divergence `asi-distinctness`.** New 3-test slice-801 (driving the real level-up cascade); fast suite green.

**Fix (slice 800): Exhaustion level 6 is fatal**

**Fix (slice 799): heavy-armor Strength-requirement speed penalty**
RAW: armor whose entry lists a Strength score (Chain Mail 13, Splint/Plate 15) reduces the wearer's speed by 10 ft unless their Strength meets it. The `strRequirement` field was authored but `getEffectiveSpeed` never read it — an under-STR character in Plate kept full speed. `getEffectiveSpeedForMode` now applies the -10 walk penalty using EFFECTIVE Strength (a STR ASI / floor / Gauntlets of Ogre Power count), built only when such armor is worn; folded into the natural base so Haste doubles the reduced speed, walk-mode only, stacks with the exhaustion penalty. No new schema or content. **Closes the [L7 audit](docs/l7-completion-audit.md) Area 6 divergence `armor-str-requirement-speed`.** New 5-test slice-799; fast suite green (no movement test regressed).

**Feat (slice 793): engine-applied 2024 background ability-score increase**

**Feat (slice 787): cast-spell `aim` enforcement — the engine owns AoE membership**
Slice 786 shipped the rasterizer; this wires it into casting. New opt-in `CastSpellIntent.aim?: {x,y}` (a point in feet): on an area spell, `planCastSpell` runs `creaturesInSpellArea` from the aim and uses the covered, line-of-effect-having creatures as the target set, ignoring `targetIds` — one rebinding at the planner entry, so every save/damage arm + `SpellCastDeclared` uses the derived set. The per-target range gate is skipped for this path (RAW: an area spell's range is to its point of origin, so a far-edge foe past the caster's range is still caught — the old gate wrongly rejected it). Without `aim`, the cast is byte-identical (additive). **Feat (slice 786): the AoE rasterizer — one canonical "who's in the cone/sphere?"**

New `creaturesInSpellArea(...)` query (`engine.query.creaturesInSpellArea`) layers state + line-of-effect on top — returns the combatant ids an area spell covers when aimed at a point (friend and foe alike, RAW). Adds the sixth 2024 shape `emanation` to `SPELL_AREA_SHAPES`. **Feat (slice 778): fuzz harness — drop-in player character (`playerCharacter`)**
